SuperDock

A free, native companion bar for your Mac's Dock. Live widgets, your AI usage limits and a board of every coding agent you have running — right next to the Dock, where you already look.

The bar sits beside the Dock, follows it as it grows, hides when it hides, and stays put when you switch Spaces.

Everything, free

AI limits & agents

Claude Code and Codex usage with reset countdowns, plus a live board of sessions: working, needs input, done. A nudge pops up when an agent finishes or needs you.

57 widgets

System stats, weather, world clocks, Now Playing, Pomodoro, to-dos, git status, local ports, crypto, Hacker News and more. Drag to reorder, resize, save as profiles.

Windows

Hover previews over Dock icons, an ⌥Tab switcher across Spaces, ⌃⌥1–9 to jump to Dock apps, and a warning with Force Quit when an app freezes.

Clipboard

History with search and filters (⌥⌘V), pinned paste slots, Copy Peek and a shelf under the notch. Password-manager copies are never stored.

Your Dock, your way

Profiles per display, Liquid Glass or solid themes, Launchpad with custom icons, live wallpapers, or replace the Dock entirely — your settings come back when you turn it off.

Plugins

Write a widget as a small JSON file: a URL and which values to show. Plugins can't run code, so they're safe to share in the community gallery.

Private by design

  • No accounts, no analytics, no crash reports sent anywhere.
  • Only widgets you add that need the internet (weather, prices, feeds) go online, and only while they're showing.
  • AI features read status, timestamps and limit percentages — never your prompts or code.
  • Permissions are asked for only when you turn on the feature that needs them.
