## SuperDock {VERSION}

A free, native companion bar for your Mac's Dock: live widgets, your AI usage limits (Claude Code, Codex), a coding-agents board, window switching, clipboard history and more. No accounts, no analytics.

**Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later.** Runs natively on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

### Install

1. Download **SuperDock-{VERSION}.dmg** below and open it.
2. Drag **SuperDock** into **Applications**.
3. Open SuperDock from Applications. The welcome window walks you through choosing where the bar goes, allowing Accessibility (needed to attach the bar to the Dock), and picking your first widgets.

### What's in this release

- **Companion bar** next to the Dock (left, right or above). It follows the Dock, hides with it, and stays put when you switch Spaces.
- **57 widgets:** AI limits, coding agents, system stats, weather, clocks, Now Playing, Pomodoro, to-dos, git status, local ports, crypto, Hacker News and more.
- **Windows:** hover previews over Dock icons, a ⌥Tab switcher, ⌃⌥1–9, and alerts for apps that stop responding.
- **Clipboard** history (⌥⌘V), pinned paste slots, Copy Peek, and a shelf under the notch.
- **Profiles,** themes, Launchpad (⌃⌥⌘L), custom icons, live wallpapers, and an optional Dock replacement mode that restores your Dock settings when it's turned off.
- **JSON plugins** and a community gallery (Settings → Plugins).
