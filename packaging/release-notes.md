## SuperDock {VERSION}

A free, native companion bar for your Mac's Dock: live widgets, your AI usage limits (Claude Code, Codex), a coding-agents board, window switching, clipboard history and more. No accounts, no analytics.

**Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later.** Runs natively on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

### Install

1. Download **SuperDock-{VERSION}.dmg** below and open it.
2. Drag **SuperDock** into **Applications**.
3. Open SuperDock from Applications. The welcome window walks you through choosing where the bar goes, allowing Accessibility (needed to attach the bar to the Dock), and picking your first widgets.

### What's new in 1.0

SuperDock 1.0 is a complete redesign. Every tile, panel and window is rebuilt on one design system, so the bar finally looks like one product.

- **A new look for every widget.** All 57 widgets share one tile kit with four sizes, and scale with your Dock from tiny to huge. Beside a side Dock the bar turns vertical.
- **Four bar styles:** Liquid Glass, Frosted, Solid and the new cream-and-ink **Paper**, each in light and dark. Reduce Transparency is respected.
- **AI Limits and Coding Agents, redrawn.** Rings and fuel-gauge bars with an 80 % mark, and an agents board where "needs input" can't be missed.
- **Meet Limo,** SuperDock's mascot. Limo pops out of the bar when an agent finishes or needs you, when an app freezes, or when a limit is close. Choose Lively, Calm or Hidden in Settings.
- **New onboarding:** four short steps with a live preview of where the bar will sit.
- **New Settings window** with a sidebar and a widget gallery: live previews, size pills and your bar as a strip you can reorder.
- **Drag to reorder** tiles on the bar, and a native right-click menu for sizes.
- **Redesigned panels:** clipboard history, Copy Peek, the notch shelf, the ⌥Tab switcher, Dock previews, a new ⌃⌥1–9 HUD, and Launchpad.
- **Now Playing works with any player:** YouTube Music, Chrome and Safari tabs, Podcasts, Apple Music, Spotify and more, with artwork and progress.
- Plugin lists now show counts (points, votes) for Hacker News, Lobsters and DEV.

Your layout, profiles and settings carry over from 0.1.
